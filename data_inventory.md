# Data Source:
Originally: European Center for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF)
Currently: National Center for Atmospheric Research (Geoscience Data Exchange)

## Data Format
Gridded data for the days September 1 1957 - August 31 2002 at hours 00:00, 06:00, 12:00, and 18:00.

For each square in the grid and each hour the data consists of 3 values: theta, u, v.

theta: the potential temperature at the z coordinate whose potential vorticity is +2 PVU
u: The speed of eastward wind at 300 hPa
v: The speed of northward wind at 300 hPa
